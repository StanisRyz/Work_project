A list field shows its options as coloured chips. Every reader of the board reads the page; whoever manages it (`can_manage`: the owner or an administrator, never on an archived board) also gets the forms — rename, kind (only while no card holds a value), «Показывать на плитке», ← →, «В архив» / «Вернуть», «Удалить» (only while unused, through the shared confirmation modal), «Сумма в колонке» for a number field (at most `max_summed` of them: each column header then reads «Σ Сумма: …») and the same for each option — and «+ Поле» at the end. Ordinary POST forms: it all works without JavaScript; `board_fields.js` only adds and removes option rows of «+ Поле» in place and hides them for a kind that is not a list. Every route asks the right again, and a refusal is a message on this page. {% endcomment %}
