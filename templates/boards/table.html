The tabs lead to the other sub-boards' tables and carry no menus — the structure is managed on the board. «Исходный срок», «Переносов» (sortable) and «Последняя причина» follow «Срок»: the first срок (— where unknown), how many times it moved, and the reason of the latest move. «Подзадачи» (`subtasks=1`, off by default) puts each card's subtasks right under it, indented, with a «Родитель» column; the Excel holds the same rows. {% endcomment %} {% include 'boards/includes/head.html' %}
