It is outside the guarded panel, so a subtask closed by a colleague never raises the conflict banner over a result being typed. The forms that add subtasks are the drawer's, below this block and in no block at all. On top, «1 из 3 выполнено» and a bar of that share (a cancelled subtask is withdrawn work and counts in neither number).
