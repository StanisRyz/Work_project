{% comment %} The board's filters, one compact row under the tabs — of the sub-board («Доска») and of its table («Таблица», `view == 'table'`) alike: `form[data-registry-filter]` of the shared `registry_tools.js` (a tick applies at once, the search after a pause), «Применить» for the no-JavaScript path. Read by `parse_board_filters()` for the page, its live fragment and the drag's counts alike. The open card and its tab stay open (the two hidden fields, kept current by `board_drawer.js` and disabled while empty); «Сбросить» — drawn only while a filter is set — drops the filter and keeps the card. «Поля» — only while the board has a live field — is a `
` of the same form (`describe_field_filters()`): a list's options as tick boxes with their colour chips and «не задано», a date's «с»/«по», a number's «от»/«до», a text's search, each named as `parse_board_filters()` reads it (`f_`, `f__from`, …). A tick and a date apply at once, a number and a text after a pause (`data-registry-delayed`); «Применить» inside it is the no-JavaScript path. The button counts the fields that filter, and the chips under the row name them, each «×» the same address without that field. «Застрявшие» (`stale=1`) keeps the open cards that have stood in their column at least its «Застой» threshold. «Доска | Таблица» are two links to the same sub-board under the same filter, and «Excel» is the table of it as a spreadsheet (`?view=table&export=xlsx`): what the table shows, with every option of the address. The table adds «Отменённые» (`cancelled=1`) and «Все поддоски» (`scope=board`) to the form and carries its order (`sort`) and the view in hidden fields; the board carries the open card and its tab instead. {% endcomment %}
