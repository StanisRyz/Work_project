{% load people %}{% comment %} One sub-board's columns — the live block `[data-live-board-columns]` (its tabs are the block beside it, `boards/includes/tabs.html`). The page and `boards:fragment` both render this file from the same `_board_context()`, and the client replaces the whole contents of the container with it — never while a card is being dragged or one of the menus below is open. Counts and cards are what the board's filter leaves (`build_board_state(filters=…)`), and every link and form keeps that filter (`filter_query`). For whoever manages the board (`can_manage`) the column headers carry their menus — `boards/includes/structure_menu.html`, a `
` of small POST forms, so they work without JavaScript; the deletes ask through the shared confirmation modal first when it is there. The `can_move_*`/`can_delete` flags only decide which buttons to draw; every route asks the service again. `data-fields-stamp` is the board's card-field setup (`selectors.fields_stamp()`): it moves this block's fingerprint with every change of «Поля карточек», even one no tile shows yet. Any reader of the board may follow a column — «🔔 Сообщать о новых карточках» in its «⋯», a working column or the closing one (`column_follow`, `can_follow_column`): a card entering it comes to the bell and the mail, and the header shows «🔔» while this reader follows it. For a reader who does not manage the board that item is the whole menu. Under the header, «Σ Кол-во: 1 250 · Сумма: 3 400 000»: the board's number fields marked «Сумма в колонке», summed over the cards the column shows under the filters (`column.sums`); nothing while the column is empty. A working column with people pinned to it («Закреплённые исполнители»: a card created here or moved here gets them) shows up to three of them in its header and «+N»; the full list is on the group's `title`. The manager sets them in the column's «⋯» menu, and «Норматив этапа» there too: a working column with a norm shows «⏱ N р.д.» in its header, and each open tile in it its traffic light (`tile.html`). «⚙» says the working column has «Правила при входе» (`column.has_rules`: pins, a checklist template, field values or followers) — a link to the column's rules page, which the manager reaches from «⋯» too. {% endcomment %}
