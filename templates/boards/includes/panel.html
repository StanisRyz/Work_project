{% load people %}{% comment %} The card panel, drawn by the server for `?card=`, `&edit=1` or `?new=`. `panel` is `view`, `edit` or `new`. The forms post to the card routes; a refusal or an invalid form comes back here with the panel open and the typed values in place (`form` is then the bound form). Closing is a plain link to the board. A `BOARD` task is worked here, not on the task page (`tasks:detail` redirects to this panel): «Выполнение» posts to `boards:card_complete`, «Вложения» is the task page's own include with its own routes, and «Вернуть в работу» posts to `tasks:reopen` through the shared confirmation modal. Every one of those rights is `tasks.permissions`' answer, carried on `card`; the board has no rules of its own about them. The contents of the live block `[data-live-board-panel]` (the `
` is the page's): the page and `boards:fragment` render this file from the same `_board_context()`. The block holds forms, so the client replaces it only when its fingerprint moved and nothing typed here is unsaved. {% endcomment %}
