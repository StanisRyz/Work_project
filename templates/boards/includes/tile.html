An open card says how long it has stood in its column — «в колонке сегодня», «в колонке 3 дн.» (`item.in_column_days`, calendar days from the journal, `selectors.in_column_since()`), the date on `title`; one past its column's «Застой» threshold (`item.is_stale`) is `board-tile--stale` with the line highlighted. A completed or cancelled card says nothing of the kind. The completion dialog of a card with open subtasks names them; «↻2» says its срок was moved twice (`item.due_change_count`, annotated likewise) (`item.open_subtask_codes`): they stay in work, the card may be finished all the same.
