{% comment %} «Вложения и комментарии» — the same two blocks, the same components and the same order as the act page: files first, discussion second. The generic `.attachment-*` and `.comment-*` components come from `components.css` and are reused as they are, so the two pages do not drift into looking different. Both forms appear only while `can_contribute` — i.e. until the protocol is archived. Archived protocols keep their files downloadable and their feed readable; they simply stop accepting new ones. Both endpoints re-check that. The two lists are separate live blocks and the forms sit outside them, so a refresh triggered by somebody else's comment can never clear a textarea somebody is typing into. {% endcomment %}
