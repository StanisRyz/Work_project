{% comment %} One assignee of one corrective action — the same markup for the first and for every one after it, which is the whole point of this partial. The two rows differ only in the *name* of the department field, and that is the stored data model, not a design choice: the first assignee's department is the corrective action's own `department` column, and the others are posted as `assignee_departments`. `to_analysis.js` re-applies exactly this rule after any add or removal, so the POST contract the Act form parses is unchanged whichever row the user deletes. `data-assignee-row` and `data-employee-pair` are what the script drives: filtering the employee list by the department beside it, keeping the split checkbox in step with how many executors are named, and — never — clearing a selection that is already there. {% endcomment %}
